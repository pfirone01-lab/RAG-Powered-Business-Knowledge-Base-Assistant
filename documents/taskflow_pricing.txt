TaskFlow — Pricing and Plans

Starter Plan — $0/month (Free)
Designed for individuals and very small teams getting started.
- Up to 5 team members
- Up to 10 active Projects
- Guest Access limited to 5 guests total
- 25MB max file upload size
- Time Tracking included, but CSV export of time reports is not available
- Basic Automations: up to 3 active automations per Project
- Community support only (no live chat or email support)

Growth Plan — $12/user/month (billed annually) or $15/user/month (billed monthly)
Designed for growing teams that need more structure and integrations.
- Unlimited team members
- Unlimited active Projects
- Unlimited Guest Access
- 25MB max file upload size
- Time Tracking with full CSV export via Reports
- Up to 15 active Automations per Project
- Slack, Google Calendar, and GitHub integrations included
- Email support with a 24-hour response target

Enterprise Plan — Custom pricing, contact sales
Designed for larger organizations with advanced security and support needs.
- Everything in Growth, plus:
- 100MB max file upload size
- Unlimited Automations per Project
- Custom onboarding and a dedicated Customer Success Manager
- Single Sign-On (SSO) and SCIM user provisioning
- Priority support with a 4-hour response target and phone support option
- Custom data retention policies (Starter and Growth retain exported data access for 30 days after cancellation; Enterprise contracts can extend this)

Add-ons (available on Growth and Enterprise only)
- Advanced Reporting Add-on: $4/user/month — adds custom dashboards and cross-project reporting beyond the default Reports tab
- Extra Storage Add-on: $10/month per additional 50GB of file storage beyond the plan default (Growth includes 100GB pooled storage; Enterprise includes 500GB pooled storage)

Billing Notes
- All paid plans include a 14-day free trial, no credit card required to start the trial.
- Annual billing is discounted approximately 20 percent compared to monthly billing on the Growth plan.
- Downgrading from Growth to Starter is only possible if your current usage fits within Starter's limits (5 members, 10 active projects); otherwise you'll be prompted to archive projects or remove members first.
- Nonprofit and education discounts of 30 percent are available on the Growth plan upon verification.

Payment Methods
TaskFlow accepts all major credit cards and, for Enterprise customers only, wire transfer or invoicing with Net 30 terms.
